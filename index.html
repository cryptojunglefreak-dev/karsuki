About KARSUKI

Built for Subaru Purists

KARSUKI supplies premium steel body panels manufactured by Zeton Garage for the most iconic Subaru models ever built. Every panel is pressed from galvanized sheet steel and shaped from precise scans of original cars.

Our focus is providing authentic, restoration quality components for Subaru enthusiasts across Asia. Every panel is engineered with hidden-flange welds and true factory geometry, so a build looks and fits exactly as it should.
